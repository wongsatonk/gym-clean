Cohesion rules

  • Filled orange = press this. Orange outline or dot = where you are now. Nothing else is orange.
  • Filled green = you achieved it. Never a button.
  • Cyan = muscles only, on every screen and in 3D.
  • Lavender bar + speaker = this line is spoken.
  • Selected / on = white fill, not orange.
  • Same CTA, cue panel, top bar and tab bar everywhere. Moods change only page black, card radius, line weight, caps and fills.
